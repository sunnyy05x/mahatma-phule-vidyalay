About Our School

Our History

Mahatma Phule Vidyalaya is a school founded by Mahatma Phule Jagruti Mandal in 1979. We offer Pre-Primary, Primary, Middle School, High School, and Higher Secondary education.

Facilities

The school features a magnificent three-storied building, spacious classrooms, modern playgrounds, fully-equipped laboratories, a rich library, and advanced e-learning facilities.
