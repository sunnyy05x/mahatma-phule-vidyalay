About Our School

Founded in 1979 by the Mahatma Phule Jagruti Mandal, Mahatma Phule Vidyalaya has been a beacon of knowledge and character-building in Bhosari. We believe in nurturing young minds to become responsible, innovative, and compassionate citizens of tomorrow.

Our Vision

To create a dynamic learning environment that inspires excellence and character development.

Our Mission

To provide quality education accessible to all, focusing on holistic development and social values.
